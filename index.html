System Capabilities

Engineered specifically for B2B quotation workflows.

InboxOps does not attempt to replace your email client or full ERP. It does one job with ruthless reliability: converting inquiries into auditable business outcomes.

📥

Smart OAuth Ingestion

Direct integration with Google Workspace and Microsoft 365. Restrict monitoring to specific folders or labels, with compound deduplication that prevents duplicate cases.

🧠

Evidence-Cited Extraction

Extracts buyer details, part numbers, quantities, units, and deadlines. Every single extracted field links directly to its source character span in the original email.

🛡️

Human-in-the-Loop Control

AI only proposes. Sales coordinators approve, edit, or reject in an ergonomic split-pane review interface designed for rapid keyboard-driven verification.

⏱️

Dual Deadline & SLA Engine

Maintains strict separation between the buyer's explicit deadline and internal employee SLAs. Evaluates working hours and workspace timezones before flagging overdue cases.

🔄

Thread-Aware Continuity

Matches customer replies via thread IDs and headers. When a customer sends revisions to a closed or resolved quote, the case is automatically re-evaluated and reopened.

🔒

Enterprise Security & Isolation

Multi-tenant PostgreSQL isolation, AES-256 token encryption, Electron context isolation, sandboxed attachment parsing, and immutable tamper-evident audit logging.

User Flow

From unread message to closed quotation in four steps.

01

Connect & Ingest

Authorise your team's sales mailbox via OAuth2. Background workers ingest new emails, normalize metadata, and enforce idempotency.

02

AI Proposes Schema

The worker categorizes the email as an RFQ or enquiry, extracts line items and requested dates, and validates data against strict Zod business schemas.

03

Coordinator Confirms

Your sales coordinator reviews the proposed fields side-by-side with the email in the desktop app and approves or edits with a single keystroke.

04

Track to Fulfillment

The case is assigned to a sales representative with an active SLA countdown, linked sub-tasks, and full auditability until marked resolved.

Enterprise Trust Architecture

Security engineered from first principles.

InboxOps processes confidential corporate procurement communications. Our threat model guarantees least privilege, sandboxed execution, and multi-tenant data boundaries.

Read Security Architecture Spec →
🛡️ Zero Prompt-Injection
Ingested emails are treated strictly as passive data. Models have zero direct database or outbound email tools.
🏢 Multi-Tenant Scoping
Every query is hard-scoped to the authenticated organization. Cross-tenant leakage is blocked at DB and API levels.
📄 Sandboxed Attachments
PDFs and spreadsheets are parsed in isolated worker subprocesses with CPU and memory bounds. No macro execution.
📜 Tamper-Evident Audit
Every assignment change, approval, and deadline adjustment produces an immutable, actor-stamped audit log.
